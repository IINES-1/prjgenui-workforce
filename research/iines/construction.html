Questions

Straight answers

Not covered here? Call or send the form below and a real person will reply.

Do I need planning permission for an extension?

Many single-storey rear extensions fall under permitted development, but it depends on the size, your location and any previous work. We check at survey and apply for a Lawful Development Certificate or full planning permission where it is needed.

Is the survey and quote really free?

Yes. We visit, measure up and send an itemised quote with no charge and no obligation. Labour, materials and provisional sums are all listed, so nothing is buried in a lump sum.

Can we stay in the house during the work?

Often, yes, for extensions and loft conversions, with dust screens and a temporary kitchen plan. Whole-house renovations are easier if you move out. We will tell you honestly which applies to your job.

What if the price changes during the build?

A fixed quote stays fixed unless you ask for changes. Any variation is priced and agreed in writing before that work starts, and provisional sums are flagged up front.

How soon could you start?

Typically four to eight weeks after the quote is accepted and approvals are in place. We give you a start date in writing and tell you early if anything moves.

Do you handle building regulations and party wall notices?

Yes. We submit building regulations applications, book the inspections and, where required, serve party wall notices and coordinate with the surveyor.
