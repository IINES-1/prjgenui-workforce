Worker Passport

One place for everything a contractor wants to see

A Worker Passport is a PRJGENUI workforce profile. It is not an official government passport or identity document.

What goes in it

Right to Work evidence, CSCS and trade cards, qualifications, training certificates, experience and work history.

What it builds

A reliability record: attendance, performance evidence and whether contractors would use you again. It grows with every job.

Who sees it

Contractors who are considering you for a job. See the privacy policy for what is shared and how long it is kept.

Tell us about you

This starts your Worker Passport. It takes a few minutes.

  1. 1
    You register. The form on this page.
  2. 2
    We get in touch. We aim to respond within one business day to talk through your experience.
  3. 3
    We check your evidence. We'll ask for your Right to Work evidence, cards and certificates and check them where a verification service is available.
  4. 4
    We match you to work. By trade, area and record.

Please don't send ID or card photos through this form. We'll tell you how to share documents safely when we speak.
