1. About these terms

These terms govern the use of prjgenuiworkforce.co.uk and the workforce services provided by PRJGENUI Workforce Ltd. By using the website or the services, you accept these terms.

2. Who we are

PRJGENUI Workforce Ltd. supplies checked construction workers and crews to contractors in Hampshire, Surrey and the South East. We are registered in England and Wales under company number 13205257. Our registered office is 124 City Road, London EC1V 2NX.

3. Our services

We check worker compliance, keep reliability records, and put forward workers and crews to painting and decorating contractors. We also build Worker Passports for individual workers. A Worker Passport is a PRJGENUI workforce profile. It is not an official government passport or identity document. The service is currently running as a pilot.

4. Worker responsibilities

Workers must provide accurate information, hold valid Right to Work and CSCS credentials, and stay compliant throughout assignments. Providing false information may result in removal from the register. The Worker Passport belongs to the worker.

5. Client responsibilities

Clients must provide accurate project requirements, make sure working conditions on site are safe, and confirm worker attendance and performance honestly. The reliability record depends on honest feedback.

6. Replacement searches

If a confirmed worker reports a no-show or late cancellation, we aim to begin the replacement search within 60 minutes during our operating hours. This is a commitment to begin the search. It is not a guarantee that a replacement will be found or will be available.

7. Pricing

Pilot pricing is confirmed in writing for each assignment before any worker is booked. Rates are not published on the website because they vary by trade, location, duration and compliance requirements.

8. Data and privacy

Use of the service is governed by our Privacy Policy. Workers and clients are responsible for the accuracy of the data they provide.

9. Liability

We check workers to the standard described in our compliance process. We are not liable for the quality of work carried out by individual workers on site. Clients are responsible for site supervision. Our liability is limited to the supply charge for the assignment in question.

10. Changes to terms

We may update these terms. Changes take effect when published on this page. Continued use of the service after changes means you accept them.
