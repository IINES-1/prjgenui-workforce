The problem

A card check tells you almost nothing

Traditional recruitment answers one question: can a body be found? Contractors are trying to answer a much longer list.

Every one of these questions can be answered if someone keeps the records. That's what we do.
  1. Will the worker turn up?
  2. Can they do the trade, or do they just say they can?
  3. Are their cards and qualifications in date?
  4. Do they hold the training this particular project needs?
  5. Have they worked in this type of environment before?
  6. Who has used them before, and would they use them again?
  7. How quickly can a whole team be on site?
  8. What happens the moment somebody doesn't show up?

Six checks, in order

Six checks before anyone reaches your site

Each check is made with the body that issued the card or certificate, and expiry dates are reviewed every day so nothing lapses quietly.

  1. 01
    Identity and Right to Work

    Checked against Home Office records. Without it there is no passport and no deployment.

  2. 02
    CSCS card

    Verified through CSCS Smart Check. Card type, status and expiry are all recorded.

  3. 03
    Trade cards and qualifications

    NVQ, City & Guilds, IPAF and PASMA, confirmed with the awarding body and recorded with their expiry dates.

  4. 04
    Training certificates

    Asbestos awareness, working at height and manual handling. If they're out of date, the worker doesn't appear in results.

  5. 05
    References

    Two references from contractors who have used the worker in the last two years, and we call them.

  6. 06
    Expiry monitoring

    Every card and certificate is tracked. Reminders go out at 60 and 30 days. Expired means blocked.

Four expiry windows, four outcomes

Blocked
Expired

Removed from all search results until renewed

Amber

Step 1

Every worker builds a Workforce Passport

Instead of a CV, each worker carries a verified record that goes with them from job to job.

  1. Cards checked with the people who issued them

    Identity, Right to Work and CSCS are confirmed with the issuing systems rather than glanced at on site. We track expiry dates so nobody gets caught out by a lapsed card.

  2. Proof of the specific skill

    Photos or a short video of the finished work, tagged to a named skill such as cutting in on satinwood or spray finishing, and signed off by the supervisor on that job.

  3. A reliability record that builds over time

    Every assignment goes on the record, whether the worker turned up or not. The Trust Score gets more accurate with every job, and it belongs to the worker rather than the agency.

Step 2

You ask for a crew and we run the checks

Tell us the trade, how many you need, the start date and what the site requires.

  1. Compliance first

    Anyone with an expired card or lapsed training doesn't appear in the results. That check runs before anything else does.

  2. Then ranked by record and fit

    Trust Score, whether the crew has worked together before, and anything you've told us you prefer. You get the strongest match rather than the first name on the list.

  3. Confirmed in writing

    Names, rates and start time are confirmed before anyone travels, so there are no surprises on Monday morning.

Step 3

If someone doesn't turn up, we start straight away

No phone tree and no waiting for a call back. A same-trade, same-area replacement search begins within 60 minutes of a confirmed no-show.

  1. T+0 minNo-show confirmed

    The site supervisor reports it through the dashboard. It goes on the absent worker's passport.

  2. T+15 minPool filtered

    Same trade, same area, cards in date, and close enough to reach the site within the shift.

  3. T+30 minRanked by Trust Score

    Workers who have worked with the rest of the crew before come first.

  4. T+45 minReplacement contacted

    We contact the top-ranked worker directly and log their acceptance and travel time.

  5. T+60 minReplacement on the way

    You see the ETA in the dashboard. The no-show stays on the record.

The 60-minute guarantee

If we haven't started a replacement search within 60 minutes of a confirmed no-show, we waive that day's supply charge. It's written into our terms.

Questions we get asked

Straight answers for contractors and for painters and decorators looking for work. Where something depends on the job, we say so.

How much do painters and decorators cost in the UK?

Agency supply rates for painters and decorators usually sit between £15 and £24 an hour, with experienced decorators in the South East towards the top of that range.

Day rates for agency labour tend to fall between £120 and £190, and specialist trades cost more. We confirm the exact rate in writing before every assignment.

Is there a shortage of painters and decorators in the UK?

Yes. CITB says the UK needs another 41,200 construction workers a year to 2030, and finishing trades such as painting and decorating are among the hardest to fill.

It's worst in the South East, where commercial, school and housing refurbishment work outstrips the number of reliable, properly checked workers available locally.

Do I need a CSCS card to work as a painter and decorator?

For almost all site work, yes. Most principal contractors won't let you through the gate without a valid CSCS card.

We check every card with CSCS rather than just looking at it, and we track expiry dates so nobody gets caught out by a lapsed card.

How do painting and decorating recruitment agencies work?

A typical agency takes a job order, rings round its list and sends whoever is free. We work differently. Compliance is checked first, then we rank the matches by Trust Score, which is built from what happened on previous jobs.

Crews who have worked together go out as a team, and if someone doesn't turn up we start a replacement search within 60 minutes.

What is a Workforce Passport?

A verified record that takes the place of a CV. It holds identity and Right to Work checks, CSCS and trade cards, qualifications, proof of skill signed off by a supervisor, and a Trust Score built from five checked factors.

The passport belongs to the worker and moves with them from job to job.

How fast can you replace a worker who doesn't show up?

A same-trade, same-area replacement search starts within 60 minutes of a confirmed no-show. We filter for workers whose cards are in date, then rank them by Trust Score, with people who have worked with the rest of the crew before coming first.

If the search doesn't start within 60 minutes, we waive that day's supply charge. The no-show also stays on the worker's passport, so the next contractor sees an honest record.
