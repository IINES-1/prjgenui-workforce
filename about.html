About

Why PRJGENUI exists

PRJGENUI Workforce grew out of Marizilena Silva Fortes's experience managing construction workers and subcontractors, and dealing with the compliance checks and last-minute problems that come with them.

The problem we set out to fix

Most recruitment stops at finding someone who is free. Before a contractor lets a worker on site, they need to know a good deal more than that.

What goes wrong

Workers don't turn up, or cancel at the last minute. Cards and training have expired or are missing. People described as experienced can't do the job. The workers who arrive aren't the ones that were agreed, and a "crew" turns out to be strangers.

What we do about it

We bring Right to Work, CSCS, training, experience and work history together in one Worker Passport. We match it against what the job actually needs. We keep a reliability record after deployment, so the next contractor sees what really happened.

How we work

01

Compliance evidence comes first

Nobody with an expired card or lapsed training is put forward for work that needs it. We check that before anything else.

02

Reliability is recorded

Every job a worker does, or doesn't turn up for, goes on their record. It belongs to the worker and is added to after every job.

03

A crew means people who have worked together

We only call a group a crew when its members genuinely have. Otherwise we say so.

04

A quick start on replacements

If a confirmed worker reports a no-show or late cancellation, we aim to begin the replacement search within 60 minutes during our operating hours. That is a commitment to start the search, not a promise that a replacement will be found.

What we check

Where an official verification service or recognised database is available, card and qualification details are checked against the relevant source and the verification evidence is recorded.

Right to Work

Evidence checked and recorded before a worker is put forward.

CSCS cards

Type, status and expiry checked at source where a service is available.

Qualifications

NVQ, City & Guilds, IPAF and PASMA, with expiry dates recorded.

Training certificates

Asbestos awareness, working at height, manual handling and site-specific training.

Work history

Previous work, references and supervisor feedback.

Reliability record

Attendance, performance evidence and whether contractors would use the worker again.

We're working towards CHAS, SafeContractor, Constructionline and REC membership. We'll add each one here once it's confirmed. We don't show badges we don't hold.

A Worker Passport is a PRJGENUI workforce profile. It is not an official government passport or identity document.
