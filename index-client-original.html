A card check tells you almost nothing

Traditional recruitment answers one question — can a body be found. Contractors are actually trying to answer a much longer list.

  • Will the worker actually arrive?
  • Can they genuinely perform the trade, not just claim to?
  • Are their cards and qualifications currently valid?
  • Do they hold the specific training this project requires?
  • Have they worked in this type of environment before?
  • Who has used them before, and would that client use them again?
  • How quickly can an entire team be mobilised?
  • What happens the moment somebody doesn't show up?

Six products, one dataset
