What changes

  • Gold brightens from #9C7A2E to #C8933A. CTAs and accents become warmer and more visible.
  • Steel blue (#4A85B0) added as a secondary accent. Used for the "For workers" panel, links, score bars, some pills, and stat numerals.
  • Section backgrounds alternate between warm white (#FAF9F6) and pale steel (#EBF2F8) instead of two nearly identical warm whites.
  • Hero stats use a mix of steel, gold, and navy instead of all navy. Adds colour variety to the most viewed part of the page.
  • Score bars fill with steel blue instead of navy. Adds colour inside the passport card.
  • Links are steel blue with gold underline. More distinctive than inherited colour.
  • Navy stays the same. No change to the primary brand colour.
