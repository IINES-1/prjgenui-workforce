# PRJGENUI Workforce — Platform Terms of Use

**Version:** 1.1 draft for review · 3 March 2026
**For approval by:** Marizilena Silva Fortes
**Note:** draft only — for legal review alongside the employment-law
opinion before the pilot; wording intentionally short and plain.

---

## 1. What these terms cover

These terms cover use of the PRJGENUI Workforce team platform at
prjgenuiworkforce.co.uk (the "platform") by the PRJGENUI team, contractors
and registered workers. They do not replace any supply agreement,
employment contract or worker contract, which govern the working
relationship itself.

## 2. Accounts and access

- Access is by invitation only. Each person has a named account and must
  keep their password confidential; admin accounts require two-factor
  sign-in.
- Invitation links and codes are personal and expire. Tell us
  (hello@prjgenuiworkforce.co.uk) if you think access has been misused.
- We may suspend or remove access where an account is misused or the
  person leaves (section 6).

## 3. What we ask of you

- Give accurate information, and correct it in your own record (or tell
  us) when it changes.
- Upload only documents you are entitled to provide (your own cards,
  certificates and identity documents), and only via the platform.
- Do not attempt to access other people's records, share your sign-in, or
  copy data out of the platform without permission (acceptable use,
  section 8).

## 4. Records and evidence

- The platform is an **operational record** for worker readiness,
  assignments and statements. It supports our checks and decisions; it does
  not replace them: statutory right-to-work checks, contracts, the records
  of issuing bodies (for example card schemes), and human decisions
  remain the authoritative sources for what they cover.
- Uploads do not become "verified" until PRJGENUI checks them.
- Every change is audited. Where something is wrong, tell us and we will
  correct it with the change recorded.

## 5. Complaints and challenges to your record

- If you disagree with anything in your record — a rating, an attendance
  record, a performance score or a readiness decision — you can challenge
  it: email hello@prjgenuiworkforce.co.uk with "Record challenge" in the
  subject line. We acknowledge within 3 working days; a named person
  reviews the challenge and responds within one month, and both the
  challenge and the outcome are audited.
- No decision about you is made solely automatically: every compliance,
  selection and deployment decision is reviewed by a person before it
  takes effect.
- You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office
  (ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint) about how we handle your personal data.

## 6. Account termination

- We may suspend or remove access if you breach these terms (including
  acceptable use, section 8), misuse the platform or another person's
  data, or stop being entitled to use it (for example when your engagement
  ends).
- Where practical we tell you first and give the reason, unless telling
  you would defeat the purpose (for example an ongoing security
  investigation).
- Ending your access does not delete your record: your data is kept (and
  can be deleted) according to the retention schedule and your rights
  under the privacy notice.

## 7. Intellectual property

- PRJGENUI Workforce Ltd owns the platform, its software and its content,
  other than anything you upload.
- You keep ownership of the documents you upload; by uploading you allow
  PRJGENUI to store, check and use them for the purposes in the privacy
  notice (including sharing verification outcomes with engaging
  contractors where that is part of the service you asked for), for as
  long as the retention schedule requires, and you confirm you are
  entitled to upload them.
- No licence is granted to misuse, scrape or copy the platform or another
  person's data.

## 8. Acceptable use

Do not, among other things:

- share sign-in details or let another person use your account;
- attempt to access records that are not yours;
- upload false or misleading documents or information;
- attempt to disrupt, probe or gain unauthorised access to the platform;
- copy, export or scrape data without permission;
- use the platform for anything unlawful.

## 9. Availability and limitations of responsibility

- We aim for continuous availability but do not promise uninterrupted
  service. Backups are taken to the standard in the baseline register
  (9.8).
- We operate the platform with reasonable care and skill, and we are
  responsible for losses we cause by failing to do that, so far as the law
  allows and so far as they were a foreseeable result of that failure. We
  are not responsible for losses caused by matters outside our reasonable
  control, or for decisions made outside the platform (the platform is an
  operational record, not the decision-maker — section 4).
- Nothing in these terms limits any statutory right you have.

## 10. Changes

We will tell registered users before material changes take effect; the
version you accepted is recorded with your account.

## 11. Governing law

England and Wales.
