## 3. Where we get your information from

- **From you** — your details, qualifications, documents and the
  corrections you make through your own passport view.
- **From engaging contractors** — the request details, assignment outcomes
  and any feedback connected to work they engaged you for.
- **From supervisors and clients on assignment** — attendance records and
  feedback about the assignment, which feed your assignment history.
- **From official verification services** — right-to-work share-code
  results and card/qualification checks, where those services are used.
- **From the platform's own assignment records** — dates, attendance and
  outcomes generated as the assignment runs.

## 4. What is required and what is optional

**Required to register and be put forward for work:** your identity and
right-to-work evidence, contact details, and the cards and qualifications
needed for the roles you want. Without these we cannot legally or
practically put you forward — we will say plainly which item is missing and
why, and nothing else is blocked by it.

**Optional:** everything else — photos, additional non-required
qualifications, and preferences (for example the kind of work or locations
you prefer). Preferences **may influence which assignments you are put
forward for**, but leaving them blank **never prevents registration and
never changes your compliance status** — no optional item affects your
eligibility, only which work you are matched to when there is a choice.

Optional consent (for example, marketing/news updates, if ever offered) is
always a separate, unticked prompt and can be withdrawn at any time without
affecting any other function. The withdrawal itself is recorded as a
separate event, with its own timestamp and wording version — alongside the
four acceptance/declaration records (privacy-notice acknowledgement,
platform-terms acceptance, legal/contractual declarations, optional
consent), each with its own version, timestamp and account identity.

## 5. How matching and performance scoring works

We use your data to put forward the workers who best fit a request. The
matching and performance evidence works like this, in plain terms:

- **Qualification match** — whether your cards and qualifications meet the
  role and site requirements, including validity (expiring, renewable or
  permanent) and whether anything has expired.
- **Assignment history** — your record of completed assignments,
  attendance, ratings and rehire decisions, and any client or supervisor
  feedback connected to those assignments.
- **Performance and reliability evidence** — scores built from factors
  such as attendance, punctuality, completing assignments and replacement
  incidents, with the current weights version recorded so the score can be
  explained and reproduced. You can see your own per-factor breakdown.

This is profiling for the purpose of matching workers to work: the
principal factors are the qualification match, assignment history and the
reliability factors above, with their recorded weights.

**No decision about you is made solely automatically.** Every compliance
decision (for example a readiness block), every selection and offer
decision, and every crew deployment is reviewed and confirmed by a named
person before it takes effect. If something in your record would hold back
your selection, a person looks at it and decides, and you are told the
reason in your own passport view.

## 6. Your rights

You can:

- ask for a copy of your data, and for corrections, erasure or
  restriction, and ask for your data to be provided to you (or, where you
  ask, to another organisation) in a structured, commonly used,
  machine-readable form (data portability), where this right applies;
- **object to processing that relies on legitimate interests** — this
  applies to the performance and reliability evidence and re-matching
  described in section 5; we will then stop that processing unless we can
  show a compelling reason that overrides your interests, and we will tell
  you either way;
- withdraw any optional consent at any time without affecting core
  functions.

**Correcting or challenging your record:** you can correct your own details
directly in your passport view; every correction is recorded. If you
disagree with anything else in your record — a rating, an attendance
record, a performance score or a readiness decision — email
hello@prjgenuiworkforce.co.uk with "Record challenge" in the subject line.
We acknowledge within 3 working days, a named person reviews the
challenge and responds within one month, and both the challenge and its
outcome are audited. If a decision was based on a score, you can ask for
the per-factor breakdown and the weights version behind it, and a human
review of the decision.

Where a legal obligation requires us to keep something (for example the
right-to-work record), we will tell you what we are keeping, why and for
how long. Where deletion happens, backup copies age out within at most 12
weeks (the backup retention period).

**Complaining to the regulator:** you can complain to the Information
Commissioner's Office, Wycliffe House, Water Lane, Wilmslow, Cheshire
SK9 5AF, telephone 0303 123 1113, or online at
ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint. You can do this at any time, and we would
also like the chance to answer the concern directly first.

## 7. Who can see your data

Access is role-checked per record: identity evidence is restricted to Owner
and Compliance; you can always see your own record. Every access-relevant
action is audited, and the audit log never contains secret values
(invitation tokens, share codes, credentials).

## 8. Who we share it with, and where it is processed

**Engaging contractors, their clients and site supervisors** receive the
limited information needed for matching and assignments — not only as
sources of information, but as recipients. What they receive:

- the worker's trade, availability and compliance status **relevant to
  their site and request requirements** (for example that required checks
  are current), and the match reasons for their request;
- assignment details: dates, site, role, attendance and outcome records
  for the assignment they engaged, and the assignment statement;
- who to contact about the worker on their site.

**What contractors never receive:** identity documents and scans —
contractors cannot access identity documents; Right to Work evidence
itself; other workers' records; internal assessments, performance scores
or per-factor breakdowns; or anything not connected to their own requests
and assignments. Contractors see only their own requests, matches and
assignments.

We use a small number of processors, listed in full in **processors.md**.
In summary: Supabase (database, sign-in, document storage — London, UK),
Vercel (website and platform hosting — limited categories: IP addresses
and request metadata/logs, on a global network that includes locations
outside the UK, covered by the safeguards in processors.md), Zoho (email
delivery — EU data centre, designated adequate under UK law), Porkbun (DNS
management) and IINES Ltd (platform development and support, acting under
a written processing agreement). We never sell your data. The encrypted
weekly backup is stored in the platform's own UK-hosted storage and is
described in the baseline register 9.8.

Where your data is processed outside the UK, the transfers and their
safeguards (adequacy or the UK Addendum/IDTA) are recorded per provider in
processors.md.

## 9. Security

Per-record access control, encrypted connections, two-factor sign-in for
admin accounts, an audited trail of every change, and backups held to the
seven-point standard in the baseline register (9.8).

## 10. Impact assessments

Before the platform is used with real personal data, we will complete a
Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA) and a Legitimate Interests
Assessment (LIA) covering the matching and scoring described in section 5,
including the profiling factors and the human-review arrangements, and we
will review the outcome with you. The drafts of both are attached to the
review pack; the commitment was approved by you on 20 October 2026.
