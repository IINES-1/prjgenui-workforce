# PRJGENUI Workforce — Platform Privacy Notice

**Version:** 1.0 draft for review · 19 October 2026
**For approval by:** Marizilena Silva Fortes
**Shown at:** first sign-in to the platform (versioned, with a recorded
acknowledgement event per GDPR schedule section 4)

This notice covers the team platform at prjgenuiworkforce.co.uk (workers,
contractors and the PRJGENUI team). The public website has its own privacy
policy. Lawful bases below are provisional pending the employment-law
opinion, per the GDPR retention and lawful-basis schedule.

---

## 1. Who we are

PRJGENUI Workforce Ltd is the controller for the personal data in the
platform. Contact: hello@prjgenuiworkforce.co.uk.

## 2. What we hold about you, why, and for how long

The full schedule — information held, purpose, lawful basis, retention
period and when it starts, and who can access it — is the **GDPR retention
and lawful-basis schedule** (gdpr-controls-proposal.md, section 2), which
this notice summarises:

| About you | Why | How long |
|---|---|---|
| Right to Work check record and any scan that forms part of it | Demonstrating the right-to-work check and its outcome (legal obligation) | 2 years after the relationship ends |
| Identity document scans (where not part of the RTW record) | Verification only | Deleted once the check is verified |
| Cards, qualifications, training (including permanent qualifications with no expiry) | Confirming site and role requirements | 2 years after expiry; 2 years after the relationship ends for permanent ones |
| Assignment history and outcomes | Delivering and evidencing assignments | 6 years after the relationship ends (reviewed first) |
| Performance and reliability evidence | Matching quality and honest records | 6 years after the relationship ends (reviewed first) |
| Consent and acknowledgement records | Proving what was agreed | 6 years after the relationship ends |
| Audit log entries (who changed what, when) | Accountability | 6 years |

"Relationship end" is defined per data class in the schedule (section 2,
"Relationship end, defined per row").

## 3. Who can see your data

Access is role-checked per record: identity evidence is restricted to Owner
and Compliance; you can always see your own record. Every access-relevant
action is audited, and the audit log never contains secret values
(invitation tokens, share codes, credentials).

## 4. Who we share it with

We use a small number of processors, listed in **processors.md**: Supabase
(database, sign-in, document storage — EU region), Zoho (email delivery —
EU region) and Vercel (website hosting — no personal data). Developer and
support access is by named, two-factor-protected accounts and is audited.
We never sell your data.

## 5. Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, corrections, erasure, restriction or
object to processing, and you can withdraw any optional consent at any time
without affecting core functions. Email hello@prjgenuiworkforce.co.uk; we
acknowledge within 3 working days and respond within one month. Where a
legal obligation requires us to keep something (for example the
right-to-work record), we will tell you what we are keeping, why and for
how long. Where deletion happens, backup copies age out within their own
retention period.

## 6. Security

Per-record access control, encrypted connections, two-factor sign-in for
admin accounts, an audited trail of every change, and backups held to the
seven-point standard in the baseline register (9.8).
